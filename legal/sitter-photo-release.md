## What this is, and what it is not

We do **not** publish sitter photographs on our website, on social media, in
advertising, or anywhere a member of the public can see them. That is our policy
and it does not change.

This form asks something much narrower: whether we may send your photograph
**privately to a family who has confirmed a booking with you**, so they know who
is at their door.

## What you are agreeing to, if you agree

- We may send one recent photograph of you, that you have provided and approved,
  **to families who have confirmed a booking with you**, and to no one else.
- We will send it once per family, together with your full name, by email.
- We tell every family the information is for arranging and briefing the sit only,
  and we ask them not to republish or share it.
- **We cannot guarantee a family will honour that.** Please decide with that in
  mind.

## What we will never do with it

- Publish it on our website
- Post it on social media
- Use it in advertising, printed material, or a pitch to a hotel or business
- Send it to anyone who has not confirmed a booking with you
- Sell it, license it, or give it to any third party

## You keep control

You own your photograph. You license us to use it only as described above, and
**you can withdraw this consent at any time** by telling us — we will stop sending
it immediately and delete the copy we hold. Withdrawing does not affect your
listing in any way and you do not need to give a reason.

We cannot claw back a photograph already sent to a family. That is worth
understanding before you sign.
