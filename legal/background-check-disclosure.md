# Disclosure and Authorization Regarding Background Investigation — DRAFT

> **DRAFT — NOT FOR USE.** Must be reviewed by a Florida attorney before use.
>
> **CRITICAL FORMATTING REQUIREMENT — READ THIS FIRST**
>
> Under the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act, this disclosure must be **clear
> and conspicuous, in a standalone document that consists solely of the
> disclosure.** In practice that means:
>
> - Print it on its **own sheet of paper**, or present it as its **own screen**.
> - Do **not** staple, combine, or bundle it with the Listing Agreement.
> - Do **not** add a liability waiver, an indemnity, an arbitration clause, or
>   any other term to this page. Courts have repeatedly found that including a
>   liability release here violates the FCRA, and it is the most common and most
>   expensive mistake employers make with this form.
> - Do not add anything decorative or promotional either.
>
> The authorization signature may sit on this same document — that is permitted —
> but nothing else may.
>
> You must also hand the person a copy of the CFPB's **"A Summary of Your Rights
> Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act"**. Download the current version from the
> Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's website; do not retype it.
>
> Delete this entire instruction box before printing.
