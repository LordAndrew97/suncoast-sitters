## 1. What we do and what we do not do

We operate a website that introduces families to independent childcare providers.
We are a **referral service**. We are not a childcare facility, an employment
agency, or your employer.

We will list you on our website, pass booking requests to you, and confirm
arrangements between you and the family. Everything after that is between the two
of you.

## 2. You are an independent contractor

You are not our employee, agent, partner, or subcontractor. Specifically:

- **You set your own rate.** We publish the rate you give us and we take no
  percentage of it. The family pays you directly.
- **You decide whether to accept any booking.** There is no minimum, no penalty
  for declining, and no obligation to be available.
- **You control how you do the work.** We do not direct or supervise the care you
  provide.
- **You may work for anyone else**, including our competitors and families you
  find yourself. There is no exclusivity.
- **You provide your own transport, equipment and supplies.**
- **You are responsible for your own taxes.** We do not withhold anything. If we
  pay you [$600] or more in a calendar year for anything, we will issue a Form
  1099-NEC.
- **You are not covered by our insurance** and you are not entitled to employee
  benefits, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance through us.

## 3. What we publish about you, and what we never publish

**We will publish:** your first name only; an illustrated avatar, not a
photograph; your years of experience; your certifications and their expiry dates;
the areas you are willing to work in; the languages you speak; the ages of
children you accept; what you will and will not do; your general availability;
your hourly rate; and a short biography you write and approve.

**We will never publish:** your photograph; your surname or last initial; your
exact age; your home address or neighbourhood; your phone number or email; the
name of your school or any employer; or anything else that would allow a member
of the public to identify or locate you offline.

**You approve your listing before it goes live.** We will not change the
substance of your biography without showing you first.

## 4. What we share privately with a family

When a family confirms a booking with you, we will send that family your **full
name**, and, **only if you have separately agreed in writing**, a photograph. We
will also confirm your certifications and provide reference details you have
authorised us to share.

We tell families this information is for arranging and briefing the sit only, and
we ask them not to republish or share it. We cannot guarantee they will comply.

## 5. Screening

You agree to complete our screening before you are listed: an in-person
interview, reference checks with at least [two] families, identity verification,
a criminal records search, a sex offender registry search, current CPR and
first-aid certification, and — if you are willing to transport children — a
driving record check.

Your authorisation for the background searches is given in a **separate
document**, the Disclosure and Authorization Regarding Background Investigation.
It is separate because federal law requires it to be.

You agree to tell us **within [5] days** if you are arrested for or convicted of
any offence, if your driving licence is suspended, or if your CPR or first-aid
certification lapses, while you are listed with us. We will re-run screening
[every 24 months] and you agree to that.

## 6. What we publish about your screening

We publish an itemised list of which checks were completed for you and in which
month. We publish the outcome only as "completed, nothing disqualifying found" or
"not applicable" with a reason. **We do not publish the contents of any report.**

## 7. Being removed from the roster

**You may ask to be unlisted at any time, for any reason, with no notice.** Email
us and we will remove your profile within one business day. You do not owe us an
explanation.

We may remove you at any time, including if a check reveals something
disqualifying, your certifications lapse, a family raises a serious concern, or
you become unreliable. Where a background report is the reason, we will follow the
adverse action process federal law requires before making it final.

Removal does not affect bookings already confirmed unless we tell you otherwise.

## 8. Your obligations to families

You agree to: arrive when you said you would; tell the family promptly if you
cannot; never leave a child unattended; never consume alcohol or drugs while
working; not transport children unless your listing says you do and you have the
parent's written consent for that occasion; not take a child swimming or into a
pool without the parent's written consent; and not post photographs of a family's
children or home on social media, ever.

You agree to complete our mandatory reporter briefing and to report suspected
child abuse or neglect as Florida law requires of every person.

## 9. Confidentiality

What you see inside a family's home stays there. Do not discuss a family's
circumstances, their children, their home or their private life with anyone other
than us where there is a safety reason to tell us.
