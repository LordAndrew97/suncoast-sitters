02  Why we built it this way

Every other agency hides the sitter.

You call, you describe your family, and someone you have never seen arrives at your door. Some agencies send you the sitter's profile only after you have already booked. We think that is backwards.

The big sites show you everyone.

Thousands of profiles, no screening, and a paywall before you can send a message. Volume is not the same as trust, and sorting strangers is not a service.

We keep the roster small on purpose.

We interview every sitter in person, check their references and background ourselves, and publish exactly what we found. If we would not leave our own kid with them, they are not on the site.

We also protect them: no photographs, no surnames, no home neighbourhoods in public. You get all of it privately when a booking is confirmed.

03  Why you are looking at a drawing

We protect our sitters too.

You will notice there are no photographs on this site, no surnames, and no home neighbourhoods. That is deliberate, and it is not us being coy.

Most of our sitters are women. Publishing a woman’s photograph beside her first name, the neighbourhood she lives in and the evenings she is free would put a searchable, permanent profile of her on the open internet. It is the single most careless thing a service like this can do, and we are not going to do it to the people who work with us.

So the public roster tells you everything about the work — the experience, the training, what each sitter will and will not do, the checks we ran and when. It tells you nothing that would let a stranger find them offline.

You still get to know exactly who is coming.

When you accept a booking, we send you privately:

  • Your sitter’s full name
  • A recent photograph, so you know who is at the door
  • Two references you can call yourself
  • Copies of their current CPR and first-aid certificates

And before a first booking we arrange a short call or a coffee, at no charge, so you meet them properly rather than on your way out the door.

Nothing is hidden from you. It is hidden from everyone else. That is a different thing, and every sitter on this roster agreed to be listed because of it.

What we ask of you in return: the details we send you are for choosing and briefing your sitter. Please do not post them, share them in a parents’ group, or pass them on. If you would rather not receive a photograph at all, tell us and we will leave it out.

04  Things we will not say to you

No guarantees.

You will not find the words 100% safe, fully vetted, risk-free or guaranteed anywhere on this site. A background check tells you what a person has been caught doing. It cannot tell you what they will do next, and any business that implies otherwise is selling you a feeling.

What we can tell you is precisely what we checked, when we checked it, and what it showed. That is a real thing, and it is worth more than a badge.

Your judgement still matters most.

Meet your sitter before the first booking — we will set it up free. Read the profile properly. Ask them the awkward questions. Watch how they are with your child in the first ten minutes.

You know your children and we do not. Our job is to make sure that every person you are choosing between is someone we would trust with ours, and then to get out of the way.

How to read a profile: each checklist item shows a tick and the month it was completed, or not applicable with the reason. If something is missing, it is missing — we do not fill gaps with reassuring language. Ask us and we will tell you why.

Privacy policy

The short version

We collect what you type into our forms and nothing else. We set no cookies and run no tracking. We do not sell or share your information. We publish no photographs and no surnames of our sitters. That is the whole of it; the detail is below.

What we collect from families

Your name, email address, phone number, the address or accommodation where a sit will take place, the number and ages of your children, and anything you choose to tell us in a notes field — which may include allergies, medical conditions or routines. We collect it because we cannot arrange childcare without it.

What we collect from sitters

Contact details, work history, references, certifications, and the information needed to run background screening, including date of birth and Social Security number. Screening is carried out by a licensed consumer reporting agency under a separate written authorisation.

What we publish, and what we never publish

Sitter profiles show a first name, an illustrated avatar, experience, certifications, areas worked, languages, capabilities, general availability, a rate, and a biography the sitter wrote and approved.

We never publish a sitter’s photograph, surname, exact age, home address or neighbourhood, phone number, email, school or employer. Publishing that combination would create a real safety risk for the people who work with us. When you confirm a booking we send you your sitter’s full name privately, and a photograph if that sitter has separately consented to it.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and contains no advertising pixels or embedded chat widgets. We use a privacy-preserving analytics tool that counts page views without cookies and without identifying anyone. There is nothing here for you to consent to, which is why you have not been asked.

What we do with your information

We use it to answer you, to arrange the booking you asked for, and to give your sitter what they need for that sit — typically your name, the address, the times, your children’s ages and your notes. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone else. Screening information goes only to our screening provider.

How we store it

Sensitive records are held in a single access-controlled, two-factor-protected store, accessible only to the two of us. Background reports and identity documents are never sent to families and never published. We destroy records securely when their retention period ends.

How long we keep it

Booking enquiries for two years, so we remember your family when you come back. Sitter screening records for as long as the sitter is listed plus a further period set by our legal obligations. Identity documents only as long as needed to complete screening. Ask us to delete your information and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Children

This site is for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we ask for a child’s age only because it determines which sitters are suitable. We never publish anything about a family’s children.

Your choices

Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email {{email}}. Sitters may ask to be unlisted at any time and we will do it within one business day, no reason needed.
